# NexaBiz ERP Marketing Kit

## Marketplace title

NexaBiz ERP — Self-Hosted Small Business ERP Template

## One-line pitch

Run sales, inventory, purchasing, production, finance, marketplace orders, reports, and
pricing from one configurable workspace you own.

## LinkedIn post

Small businesses often outgrow spreadsheets before they are ready for a large enterprise
system.

NexaBiz ERP is a self-hosted template for teams that need a practical operating system
for daily work:

- sales and customer management
- products, variants, and inventory
- purchasing and supplier workflows
- production and BOM tracking
- finance, expenses, and reports
- marketplace order import
- HPP, pricing, and break-even tools

Configure the language, currency, locale, and business identity during setup. Deploy it
on your own machine, VPS, or cloud account and keep control of your data.

Built for UMKM, online sellers, workshops, small manufacturers, and growing teams.

## Feature bullets

- Self-hosted React + FastAPI + MongoDB stack
- Role-based access for owner, admin, production, and finance teams
- Responsive dashboard with KPI, chart, and low-stock visibility
- CSV exports and marketplace import workflows
- Onboarding wizard for business identity and opening balances
- Configurable currency and regional formatting
- Demo seed data for a faster evaluation
- Quick Sale mode with cash, transfer, QRIS, e-wallet, and credit payment options
- Printable receipt and owner-friendly daily cashflow cards
- Light CRM customer history and WhatsApp contact shortcut

## Buyer expectations

This product is software source code, not a hosted service. The buyer is responsible for
hosting, database setup, domain, TLS, email provider, backups, and production security.

Before publishing, add the final commercial license, support policy, version number, and
screenshots to the marketplace listing.
